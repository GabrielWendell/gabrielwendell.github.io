About

I am a PhD student in Physics at Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Norte (UFRN). My research explores the intersection of topological methods, machine learning, and complex physical systems, with applications ranging from astrophysics to condensed matter.

My work involves both theoretical modeling and computational implementations, with a strong emphasis on reproducible research and open-source software.

Selected Projects

(XXXIX EFNNE) TDA Diagnostics of CNN Predictors on Shock Tube Dynamics

Here we use TDA to evaluate a baseline 1D CNN trained to predict the temporal evolution of the Brio-Wu MHD shock tube, a classical benchmark containing shocks, rarefactions, and discontinuities. Persistent homology and Betti-number signatures extracted via spatial Takens embeddings reveal that the CNN fails to preserve the multiscale topological structure of the true solution.

GitHub

(4th DSML) TDA for Gravitational-Wave Detection under Low-to-Noise Ratios

A project that investigates how Topological Data Analysis (TDA) techniques can identify gravitational-wave-like signals embedded in strong noise. Using synthetic datasets, the workflow reconstructs the phase-space topology of signals and tracks persistent homological features (connected components and loops) as the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) varies.

GitHub

(XLVIII RASAB) TDA and SSL for Galaxy Morphological Analysis

Application of Topological Data Analysis (TDA) and Self-Supervised Learning (SSL) to investigate the unsupervised clustering of galaxies from the Galaxy Zoo 2 (GZ2) dataset. The objective is to evaluate whether topological features combined with learned SSL embeddings can reveal meaningful astrophysical structures.

GitHub

PyMoS2 - Python Modules for a Static Star Model

A personal Python library providing mathematical and computational tools to simulate the interior of a static, non-magnetic star of 1 solar mass, from the production of energy through nuclear reactions, the radiative and convective transport of energy and the movement of a convective cell.
